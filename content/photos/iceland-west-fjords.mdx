# Iceland, West Fjords

The least visited part of Iceland for good reason — it's hard to get to, the roads are rough, and there's not much out there except landscape. Which is exactly why it's worth going.

## What We Found

Long stretches of gravel road hugging cliff edges. Waterfalls with no names and no one around to see them. A red sand beach that felt like Mars. Seals watching us from rocks offshore.

The light at 11pm looked like 7pm. Time felt negotiable.

We drove for hours without seeing another car. Just mountains, ocean, and the kind of silence that makes you realize how much noise you usually carry around.
