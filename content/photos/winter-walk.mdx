# Winter Walk

There's a particular quality to winter light — thin, clear, almost blue. Everything feels sharp and quiet. Bare trees become line drawings against pale sky.

I walked through the neighborhood park today, camera in hand, mostly just observing. Not hunting for shots, just being present with the season.

## Winter as Metaphor

Winter strips things down to their essentials. No leaves to hide behind, no abundance to distract. Just structure and negative space.

Maybe that's why I find winter compelling as a subject — it mirrors the kind of simplicity I'm drawn to in design and code. Remove the ornament, see what's left.

Early thoughts on seasonal observation and what winter reveals when summer's noise is gone.
