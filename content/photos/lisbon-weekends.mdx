# Lisbon Weekends

Three long weekends in Lisbon over the fall. The kind of city where you can get lost for hours just looking up at buildings — blue and white tiles covering entire facades, laundry hanging from wrought-iron balconies, streets so steep you take the tram just to avoid the climb.

## Neighborhoods

- Alfama's narrow alleys and fado music drifting from doorways
- Bairro Alto at sunset, the whole city turning gold
- LX Factory's industrial-chic cafes and bookstores
- Belém's waterfront and impossible pastries

The light here is something else. Warm and soft even in late afternoon. The city feels like it's perpetually bathed in that hour before sunset.

Best discovery: tiny neighborhood tascas where locals eat lunch and no one speaks English. Just point at what looks good and trust the process.
