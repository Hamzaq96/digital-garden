# Tokyo, Spring

A week wandering through Tokyo during cherry blossom season. The city unfolds in layers — ancient temples tucked between skyscrapers, quiet residential streets giving way to neon-lit intersections, parks where salarymen eat lunch under blooming sakura trees.

## Places Visited

- Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden
- Shibuya Crossing at night
- Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa
- Quiet backstreets of Shimokitazawa
- Tsukiji Outer Market

The light was different than I expected. Softer. Even in the middle of the chaos.
