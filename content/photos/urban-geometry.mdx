# Urban Geometry

Cities are built on grids and angles. Walking through downtown, I started noticing how buildings create visual rhythms — repeating windows, intersecting lines, the play of shadow and concrete.

## Finding Patterns

There's a satisfying quality to architectural photography. It's about reduction — taking the complexity of urban space and finding the essential geometry underneath.

I'm drawn to:
- Clean lines and repetition
- The contrast between old and new structures
- How light carves shapes out of buildings
- Negative space between structures

## Technical Notes

Shot at midday when shadows are sharpest. High contrast works here — the geometry reads better with strong blacks and whites. Still learning how to balance composition with the messiness of real urban environments.

This note is budding — I've started developing a visual language for architectural work, but there's more to explore around how urban spaces shape movement and attention.
