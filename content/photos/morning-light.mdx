# Morning Light

The way light moves through space has always fascinated me. This morning, the sun came through the kitchen window at just the right angle, creating patterns on the wall that lasted maybe five minutes.

There's something meditative about catching these moments — they're fleeting, unrepeatable, and entirely ordinary. But that's what makes them worth noticing.

## On Light and Space

Light transforms space. The same room can feel completely different at 7am versus 7pm. I'm learning to pay attention to these shifts, to notice how environment affects mood and focus.

This is a seedling note — early observations about light, space, and attention. More to come as I explore this further.
